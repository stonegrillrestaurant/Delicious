Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Courtside Gatherings ("Courtside", "we", "us", or "our") provides an online booking, payment-verification, court scheduling, and venue queue system for customers and authorized staff. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Your name, Google account email address, and authentication identifier.
  • Mobile number and booking contact information.
  • Court, date, time, booking type, and Open Play registration details.
  • Names of actual players included in an Open Play payment or registration.
  • GCash or other payment reference numbers and customer-submitted payment receipt images.
  • Payment verification status, booking status, queue assignment, and entrance/check-in status.
  • Basic technical or security information needed to operate and protect the system.

How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate customers, staff, and administrators.
  • Display court availability and prevent overlapping bookings.
  • Process Open Play registrations and private court rental requests.
  • Match customer-submitted payment information with merchant-side payment records.
  • Place verified Open Play players in the appropriate venue queue.
  • Support entrance verification and check-in.
  • Maintain booking, payment, security, and operational records.

Google Sign-In

Courtside may use Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication. We use Google account information only as needed to identify the signed-in user and provide access to the appropriate booking or administrative functions.

Payment Information

Courtside may collect a payment reference number and a receipt screenshot or photo submitted by the customer. These are used to verify that a payment corresponds to a booking or Open Play registration. Courtside does not use the customer booking page to collect a bank password, GCash PIN, or full payment-card number.

Player Names and Entrance Verification

When one payment covers multiple Open Play participants, the payer may be asked to provide the actual names of the players. Venue staff may compare a player's presented identification with the registered name before marking that player as checked in.

The current system is intended to record the verification/check-in result rather than store a copy of a player's physical identity document, unless a separate notice is provided for a future feature that specifically requires such storage.

Service Providers

Courtside uses third-party services necessary to operate the system. These may include Google and Firebase services for authentication and database functions, and Google services used for receipt, spreadsheet, or operational record handling. Payment transactions may also involve the customer's chosen payment provider, such as GCash.

Information Sharing

Courtside does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be accessed by authorized Courtside personnel when necessary for booking, payment verification, venue operations, security, accounting, or legal compliance. Information may also be processed by service providers that support the operation of the system.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including authenticated access and role-based restrictions for sensitive administrative functions. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited according to operational need.

Data Retention

Booking, payment-verification, and operational records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, accounting, security, dispute resolution, audit, and legitimate business purposes. Records may be archived or deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your Choices

You may contact Courtside Gatherings if you have a question about information associated with your booking or account, or if you wish to request correction or deletion where applicable and consistent with legitimate recordkeeping obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Courtside system or our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
