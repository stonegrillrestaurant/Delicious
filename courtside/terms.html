Open Play

  • Open Play sessions are scheduled by authorized Courtside staff or administrators.
  • The applicable price per player and maximum number of players are shown in the booking system.
  • When one payment covers multiple players, the actual names of all players must be entered correctly.

  • Verified player names may be automatically added to the appropriate court queue.

Private Court Rental

  • Courts 1–4 are normally charged at ₱250 per hour.
  • Courts 5–6 are normally charged at ₱350 per hour.

  • Open Play sessions and existing reservations block overlapping private rental times.
  • A verified private rental blocks the selected court and time range from further booking.

Walk-in payments handled directly by venue staff may use a separate on-site workflow. Online payment verification and walk-in payment handling may appear in the same queue system but are treated as separate payment sources.
